
## Fixed Point Representation
**Represent the decimal number in fixed-point binary:**
   - 12.75 with 4 bits for the integer part and 4 bits for the fractional part. Ans: Integer part: 12 = 1100 (4 bits) Fractional part: 0.75 = .1100 (4 bits)
   - 5.125 with 3 bits for the integer part and 5 bits for the fractional part. Ans: Integer part: 5 = 101 (3 bits) Fractional part: 0.125 = .00100 (5 bits)
   - 7.5 with 4 bits for the integer part and 4 bits for the fractional part. Ans: Integer part: 7 = 0111 (4 bits)
Fractional part: 0.5 = .1000 (4 bits)
