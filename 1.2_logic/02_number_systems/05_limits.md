
## Limits of Binary Representation
**Determine the limits of binary representation:**
   - Largest positive number with 8 bits in unsigned binary Ans: Binary: 11111111 = 255
   - Largest positive number with 8 bits in signed binary (two's complement) Ans: Binary: 01111111 = 127
   - Smallest negative number with 8 bits in signed binary (two's complement) Ans: Binary: 10000000 = −128
