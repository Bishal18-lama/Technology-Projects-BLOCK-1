## Two's Complement
**Find the two's complement of the binary number:**
   - 101010,
      Step 1: 1's complement → 010101
      Step 2: 2's complement → 010101 + 1
         Answer: 010110
   - 110011,
      Step 1: 1's complement → 001100
      Step 2: 2's complement → 001100 + 1
         Answer: 001101
   - 1001,
      Step 1: 1's complement → 0110
      Step 2: 2's complement → 0110 + 1
         Answer: 0111
