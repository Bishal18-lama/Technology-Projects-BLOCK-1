## Reflection
Why are many components integrated into a single board in embedded computers such as the Raspberry Pi?
Write **100 words**.
                             Ans: Embedded computers, such as the Raspberry Pi, are made smaller, more affordable, and more energy-efficient by integrating numerous components into a single board. All of the components can function together on a single, small board rather of being connected by numerous wires. Devices with small spaces and low power requirements can benefit from this. Additionally, it facilitates the Raspberry Pi's portability, installation, and application in many projects. I discovered that these parts work together to enable the Raspberry Pi to carry out a variety of functions, including managing sensors, executing software, and establishing connections with other devices.
