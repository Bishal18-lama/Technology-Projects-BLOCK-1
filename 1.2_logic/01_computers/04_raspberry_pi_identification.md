## Reflection
Why are many components integrated into a single board in embedded computers such as the Raspberry Pi?
Write **100 words**.
                             Ans: Embedded computers, such as the Raspberry Pi, are made smaller, more affordable, and more energy-efficient by integrating numerous components into a single board. Additionally, having everything on one board eliminates the need for additional hardware and wiring. This facilitates the device's installation and use in various tasks. I discovered that despite its tiny size, the Raspberry Pi is capable of carrying out a wide range of functions, including connecting to other devices, operating sensors, and executing applications. Because embedded systems sometimes have limited space, power, and resources while still needing to dependably do particular tasks, this design is particularly helpful.
